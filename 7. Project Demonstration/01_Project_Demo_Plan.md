# EduGenie Project Demonstration Plan

EduGenie Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

## Demonstration Objective

The objective of the demonstration is to show how EduGenie uses Google Gemini to provide educational support through question answering, concept explanation, quiz generation, text summarization, and personalized learning roadmaps.
