# EduGenie Problem Statements

## Problem Statement 1

Students often struggle to understand complex educational concepts because online explanations may be too advanced, too lengthy, or not suitable for their learning level.

## Problem Statement 2

Learners often search multiple websites to find answers, examples, practice questions, and study material, which makes the learning process slow and confusing.

## Problem Statement 3

Self-learners may not know which topics to study first or how to create a structured learning plan for a subject.

## Problem Statement 4

Educators spend considerable time preparing quizzes, explanations, and summary material for their students.

## Final Problem Statement

Students, self-learners, and educators need an accessible AI-powered educational assistant that can answer questions, explain concepts, generate quizzes, summarize learning material, and create personalized learning roadmaps.

## Proposed Solution

EduGenie solves this problem through a lightweight web application built with FastAPI, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Google Gemini.
