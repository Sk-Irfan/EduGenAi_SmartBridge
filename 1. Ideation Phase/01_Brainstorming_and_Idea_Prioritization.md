# EduGenie Brainstorming and Idea Prioritization

## Project Name

EduGenie Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

## Problem Ideas

| Idea | Problem Solved | Impact | Effort | Priority |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AI question answering | Learners need quick explanations | High | Medium | 1 |
| Concept explanation | Difficult topics are hard to understand | High | Medium | 2 |
| Quiz generation | Learners need self-assessment | High | Medium | 3 |
| Text summarization | Study materials are lengthy | High | Medium | 4 |
| Learning roadmap | Learners do not know what to study next | High | Medium | 5 |
| Voice assistant | Learners want hands-free learning | Medium | High | 6 |

## Selected Idea

EduGenie was selected because it combines question answering, concept explanation, quiz generation, text summarization, and personalized learning roadmaps in one educational assistant.

## Target Users

- School students
- College students
- Self-learners
- Teachers
- Educational content creators

## Value of the Idea

EduGenie helps users learn faster by providing simple explanations, practice questions, summaries, and structured learning plans using Google Gemini.
