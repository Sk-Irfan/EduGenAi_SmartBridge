# EduGenie Empathy Map

## Student

### Thinks

- I want to understand difficult topics quickly.
- I need explanations that match my learning level.
- I want to practice before an examination.

### Feels

- Confused by complicated explanations.
- Nervous before examinations.
- Frustrated when searching many websites.

### Says

- Can someone explain this in simple language?
- Can I get practice questions?
- What is the correct answer?

### Does

- Searches online.
- Watches educational videos.
- Uses notes and textbooks.
- Practices before tests.

### Pain Points

- Difficult terminology.
- Too much study material.
- Lack of personalized practice.
- No structured learning plan.

### Goals

- Understand concepts.
- Improve examination performance.
- Study efficiently.

---

## Self-Learner

### Thinks

- I need a clear learning sequence.
- I want to know what to study next.
- I need practical exercises.

### Feels

- Overwhelmed by too many resources.
- Motivated when progress is visible.
- Uncertain about the correct learning path.

### Says

- Create a roadmap for me.
- What should I learn first?
- How can I become job-ready?

### Does

- Studies independently.
- Searches courses and tutorials.
- Practices through projects.

### Pain Points

- No personalized guidance.
- Difficulty tracking progress.
- Unclear subject progression.

### Goals

- Build strong fundamentals.
- Learn intermediate and advanced topics.
- Become confident in a subject.

---

## Educator

### Thinks

- I need to prepare learning material quickly.
- Students have different learning levels.
- I need useful practice questions.

### Feels

- Pressured by preparation time.
- Responsible for student understanding.
- Satisfied when students improve.

### Says

- Generate a quiz for this topic.
- Explain this concept simply.
- Give me a summary for students.

### Does

- Creates lessons.
- Prepares assessments.
- Explains concepts.
- Reviews student progress.

### Pain Points

- Manual content creation takes time.
- Students have different needs.
- Repeatedly preparing similar materials.

### Goals

- Save preparation time.
- Support different types of learners.
- Improve student outcomes.
